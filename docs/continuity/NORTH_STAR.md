# NORTH STAR — what Validexia is becoming

Validexia is an **AI-native Third-Party Risk Management operating platform** — not a
vendor-risk application with a chatbot attached.

The platform's direction, in one paragraph: automate the vast majority of repetitive
vendor-risk work; understand the entire vendor-risk lifecycle; run specialised AI
capabilities across every major module on **one** orchestration engine; proactively
research vendors and external developments; continuously monitor change and emerging
risk; connect vendors, evidence, assessments, incidents, dependencies and business
services into one evidence graph; predict and investigate emerging risk where evidence
and validated models permit; execute permitted tasks reliably; and keep humans in
control of consequential decisions.

## Operating principles (founder-set, do not dilute)

- **One engine.** All AI execution runs on the merged AgentEngine. No second
  orchestrator, no disconnected per-module chatbots.
- **Evidence or it didn't happen.** Every AI conclusion carries a source reference.
  A conclusion without provenance fails validation. Risk scores are computed by
  deterministic code — an LLM never invents a score or a probability.
- **Three autonomy modes.** Assist (recommend), Approval (prepare, human approves),
  Autonomous (routine authorised actions only). Sensitive writes always require a
  human. The AI never modifies its own permissions.
- **Confirmed is not the same as detected.** An external event (a CVE, a breach headline)
  is not a customer's exposure until the relationship is established in data. The data
  model carries confirmed / unconfirmed / ruled-out states.
- **Honesty about state.** Proposed, mocked, partially implemented or untested
  capabilities are never described as functional. The frontend today is a prototype
  over fixtures; the backend is real but young. We say so.
- **Founder authority.** Vision, architecture, security boundaries, risk methodology
  and roadmap priorities change only with founder approval.

## The differentiation we are building toward (evidence-based, 2026-11-13)

Competitive research (see RESEARCH_REGISTER.md) found the crowded ground — continuous
monitoring, questionnaire automation, outside-in ratings — is already GA at multiple
incumbents. The open ground we aim at:

1. **Independent agent verification + published agent accuracy.** No researched
   competitor verifies one agent's output with an independent agent, and none
   publishes accuracy evidence for AI agents.
2. **Per-vendor risk trajectory outlooks** with horizon and stated uncertainty,
   scored later against outcomes (a forecast accountability ledger).
3. **The combination** — Foresight signals + living evidence graph + dependency-aware
   scenario simulation (Risk Flight Simulator) — delivered by nobody today; an
   integration moat with a finite window, not a permanent position.
